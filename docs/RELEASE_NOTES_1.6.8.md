# Niratan Mac 1.6.8

## 中文

- 视频字幕搜索现在聚合 AJATT、Jimaku 和 OpenSubtitles，自动收集并按语言和版本整理字幕，可筛选后直接使用。
- 动画作品支持原名、英文名和罗马音匹配及季度选择；来源配置统一收进字幕设置。
- 歌词模式的横排长句会按内容自动换行并完整参与选择与高亮；窗口或字体变化时，末尾文本也不会丢失。

## English

- Video subtitle search now aggregates AJATT, Jimaku and OpenSubtitles, collecting selectable subtitle files grouped by language and release version.
- Anime searches support original, English and romaji titles with season selection; provider configuration is consolidated in subtitle settings.
- Long horizontal lyrics wrap to their full height and remain fully selectable and highlightable, including after window or font changes.
